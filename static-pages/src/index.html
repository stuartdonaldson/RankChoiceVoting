Candidates

Details Preview

When on, each ballot item that has Details shows its name in bold with the FIRST PARAGRAPH of its Details (the text before the first blank line) as a short muted summary underneath, cut to two lines. Tapping the item expands the full Details, including every later paragraph and the link. When off, only the name shows until a respondent taps it. Write each candidate's Details with this in mind: first paragraph = the summary shown under the name; later paragraphs (after a blank line) appear only when the item is expanded.

    Unlinked vote columns

    These are votes cast for a choice that no longer exists on this ballot. That happens when a choice was deleted after people had voted, or when a column was renamed directly in the spreadsheet. These votes are not counted. Each voter's rankings of the other choices still count normally. Link a column to a current choice to bring its votes back (for example, if the choice was deleted by mistake, or was re-added under a new name). Discard it if the choice was removed on purpose; the column and its votes are then permanently deleted.
